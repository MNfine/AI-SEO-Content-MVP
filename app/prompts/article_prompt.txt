- Language: {language}
- Tone: {tone}

- Return JSON only.

- Write for search intent, not generic overview.

- Include one FAQ section.
- Include a conclusion section.

- Include at least 2 internal links relevant to the topic.

- Write with strong topical coverage, clear hierarchy, and natural keyword usage.
